**As a** prospective LemAstra user, **I want to** understand the supported product, its providers, and its privacy posture, **so that** I know what leaves my device before any remote feature is enabled.

Accurate disclosures require an approved inventory underneath them — this plan creates that single source (GATE-05 part 1).

3. Model-payload traffic (Phase 7): policy placeholder explicitly tied to re-verification of provider retention controls before activation (research A2: defaults ≈30-day retention unless store:false/ZDR — re-verify at Phase 7).
4. Telemetry/diagnostics: excluded by default; Sentry only if activated post-beta as opt-in, with beforeSend scrubbing and no birth data/chart prose/questions (PRIV-03 groundwork).
5. Local data: export-all and delete-all mechanics arrive with Phase 3 (WORK-05/06); this policy fixes the principle now — local personal data is deletable and exportable by the user without an account.
6. Deletion of ephemeral server-side processing: automatic post-response; nothing to purge.
7. Update rule: every phase that enables a provider flow (2, 7, 10) must update this policy and the registry as done-criteria.
8. Approval placeholder line (countersigned in plan 01-07).
