## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** prospective LemAstra user, **I want to** understand the supported product, its providers, and its privacy posture, **so that** I know what leaves my device before any remote feature is enabled.

"No secrets in the client" must be enforced, not promised — this plan installs the classification policy and the scanning gate (GATE-06).

<objective>
Write the client secret-isolation policy (Pattern 2 classification), configure gitleaks with a custom EXPO_PUBLIC_ secret-name rule and a rationale-contract allowlist, and prove the repo history, working tree, and exported web bundle all scan clean.

Purpose: GATE-06 requires that no model, calculation, database, or third-party service secret ships in the mobile clients; EXPO_PUBLIC_ inlining makes bundle-time scanning the authoritative check.
Output: docs/governance/secret-isolation-policy.md, .gitleaks.toml, .gitleaksignore, green local scans.
</objective>
